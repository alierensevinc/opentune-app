Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of Your information when You use the OpenTune application ("Service") and tells You about Your privacy rights and how the law protects You.

1. Definitions

  • Application refers to OpenTune, the software program provided by the Company.
  • Company (referred to as "We", "Us", or "Our") refers to OpenTune (Turkey).
  • Device means any device that can access the Service such as a smartphone or digital tablet.
  • Personal Data is any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Usage Data refers to data collected automatically, either generated by the use of the Service or from the Service infrastructure itself.

2. Microphone Access & Audio Processing

OpenTune requires access to Your Device's microphone solely to detect the pitch and frequency of your musical instrument in real time. Audio input is processed locally on Your Device in real time and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted to Our servers or any third party.

3. Collecting and Using Your Data

Usage Data & Analytics

When You access the Service through a mobile device, certain diagnostic and usage information may be collected automatically, including your mobile device type, operating system version, anonymous device identifiers, and crash/diagnostic logs used to maintain and improve app stability.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

We may use third-party Service Providers such as Google AdMob to display advertisements within the Application. Where required by law (including iOS App Tracking Transparency), We request Your permission before any advertising identifier is used for personalized ads.

4. Retention & Deletion of Data

We retain diagnostic and usage data only for as long as necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy (up to 24 months for usage statistics and security logs), or as required to comply with legal obligations. You may contact Us at any time to request access to or deletion of any Personal Data associated with You.

5. Security of Your Data

The security of Your data is important to Us, and We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect it. Remember that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 16. If You are a parent or guardian and become aware that Your child has provided Us with Personal Data, please contact Us so We can remove that information.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update Our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify You of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us by email at sevinc.aeren@gmail.com.
